01

I don't code and don't have a laptop. Can I still do this?

Yes. A free, open-source Android phone app does the same cleanup over Wi-Fi.

02

Is this safe? Can it brick the TV?

Nothing is flashed, rooted or deleted; packages are disabled in the user profile. Lists are applied in small batches with a restore script ready, and the TV's own Settings → Apps page can re-enable anything.
