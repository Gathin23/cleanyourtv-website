Three steps. Your coding assistant does the typing.

  1. 01
    On the TV

    Switch on Wi-Fi debugging in Developer options.

  2. 02
    On your computer

    Paste the prompt into your coding assistant. It finds the TV on your Wi-Fi.

    Follow along

    Approve on the TV, pick a theme. One command undoes it all.
