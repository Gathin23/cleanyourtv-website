03 · How it works

Careful by default.

Safe by design

  • Plan first. Nothing runs until you have seen it and tapped.
  • Restore point saved on the phone before the first change.
  • Nothing uninstalled. Apps are only switched off.
  • Undo anything, one app or everything.
  • A watchdog on the TV puts its own home screen back if the phone doesn't confirm within 150 seconds.

What it keeps

  • Your apps: Netflix, YouTube, Prime Video, live TV and HDMI inputs.
  • The basics: Wi-Fi, remote, keyboard and the parts Android needs.
  • Casting, the voice assistant and software updates, unless you switch them off.

What you need

  • An Android phone on Android 9 or newer, with a screen lock.
  • An Android TV or Google TV on the same Wi-Fi.
  • Network debugging on in the TV settings. The app shows where, and switches it off again at the end.

Not for Samsung, LG or Roku. Those TVs don't run Android, so the app can't work on them.
